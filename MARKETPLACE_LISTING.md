## Full description
LexRTL is a premium, right-to-left Hebrew landing page designed to turn visitors into leads. It's purpose-built for law firms, but works for any professional service (accountants, consultants, clinics).

Unlike most templates, **all content lives in one file** — `src/config/site-config.ts`. Edit your firm name, services, testimonials, FAQ, colors, and contact details in one place and the whole site updates. No hunting through components.

It ships with a working **contact form that saves leads to Firebase** and sends you an **EmailJS notification**, plus privacy / terms / accessibility pages, a cookie-consent banner, an accessibility widget, and SEO metadata — everything a real, compliant Israeli business site needs.

### Features
- 🇮🇱 **Full Hebrew RTL** — proper right-to-left layout, Rubik font
- 🗂️ **One-file content config** — rebrand the entire site from `site-config.ts`
- 📥 **Lead capture** — contact form → Firebase Firestore
- 📧 **Email notifications** — EmailJS on every new lead
- 🎨 **Theme colors** as simple config values
- ⚖️ **Legal pages included** — privacy, terms, accessibility
- ♿ **Accessibility widget + cookie consent** (Israeli-compliance friendly)
- 🔍 **SEO-ready** — metadata, keywords, semantic markup
- 📱 Fully responsive · sections: hero, features, services, about, testimonials, FAQ

### Tech stack
Next.js (App Router) · TypeScript · Tailwind CSS · Firebase Firestore · EmailJS · Lucide icons

### What you need
