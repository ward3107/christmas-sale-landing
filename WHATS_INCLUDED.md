# What's Included

A complete, production-ready Hebrew / RTL landing page template for a commercial law firm, built on Next.js 15, Tailwind CSS, and Firebase. Buy it once, customize it in minutes, and launch.

## What You Get

- **Full source code** — a clean, well-structured Next.js 15 (App Router) project in TypeScript.
- **Single-file content control** — every piece of copy, contact detail, and theme color lives in `src/config/site-config.ts`. No need to dig through components.
- **Complete page sections** — hero, features, services, about, testimonials, navbar, and footer, all styled for right-to-left Hebrew.
- **Lead capture, wired up** — a contact form that saves submissions to Firebase Firestore and sends you an email notification via EmailJS.
- **Legal pages included** — ready-to-edit Hebrew Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and Accessibility Statement.
- **Accessibility widget & cookie consent** — built in and configurable.
- **Floating social / contact widget** — call, WhatsApp, and social buttons.
- **SEO-ready** — configurable metadata, title, description, and keywords.
- **Security layer** — client-side CSP, form rate limiting, and violation tracking.
- **Tests** — Vitest unit tests and Playwright e2e tests included.
- **Documentation** — a full `README.md` with setup, customization, and deployment instructions.

## Ideal Use Cases

- Solo lawyers and boutique law firms that need a professional Hebrew web presence fast.
- Agencies and freelancers building sites for legal or professional-services clients.
- Any Hebrew / RTL business landing page — the law-firm content is easy to swap for another vertical.
- A learning base for a modern Next.js 15 + Firebase + RTL project.

## What You Must Provide

To go live you will need to supply a few of your own (free-tier) accounts and details:

- **A Firebase project** — for storing lead-form submissions in Firestore. Free Spark plan is sufficient to start.
- **An EmailJS account** — for email notifications when a lead submits the form. Free tier includes 200 emails/month, no credit card required.
- **Your own content and branding** — business name, contact details, copy, and colors (all edited in `src/config/site-config.ts`).
- **A hosting account** — Vercel is recommended and free for this project.

## Not Included

- Domain name and hosting fees (if any).
- Logo or custom graphic design assets.
- Legal review of the boilerplate legal pages — please have them reviewed by a qualified professional before publishing.
- Ongoing support or custom development beyond the delivered code.
